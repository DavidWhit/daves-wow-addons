# Dave's Map (daves_map)

QuestieDB's **WoW: Forever** tracked quest objectives, ready turn-ins, optional POI layers, and tab-driven **Zone** & **Dungeon** markers drawn on a taint-free world map layer, paired with a left-rail **`Base` / `Tracker` / `Zone` / `Dungeon`** sidebar, Ready-to-Turn-In & zone quest tracker, and OpenMap `v0.8.10`'s seamless stitched world map and multi-floor dungeon/raid maps.

Targets: WoW: Forever (`## Interface: 16001`). Uses two tracked companion addons in `wowaddons/` (also packaged separately on `releases`):
- **`daves_map_data`**: QuestieDB Forever pins, objectives, turn-ins, clean sequential quest chains (`D.pre` & `D.nxt`), quest item rewards (`D.rew` & `D.iname`), structured rare monster drops with drop percentages (`D.drops`), zone level brackets, and per-zone dungeon & dungeon-quest lists (`D.dungeons`) (`tools\Update-MapData.py`)
- **`daves_map_textures`**: OpenMap `v0.8.10` seamless world & dungeon map art and tables (`tools\Update-MapTextures.py`)

## Use

- **Single Unified Window (`[ Map Column (Dropdowns + Centered Zone Stage) | Splitter | Middle 46px Tab Rail | Right Content Pane ]`, `MapChrome.lua`, `MapStage.lua` & `Panel.lua`)**:
  - Replaces Blizzard's stone/portrait World Map border, brown `NavBar` breadcrumb tabs, floor overlay dropdown, default `QuestMapFrame` side panel, `SidePanelToggle`, and canvas tracking/pin buttons with one unified HUD Edit Mode translucent dialog window (`DavesMapWorldChrome`, `miniWorldMap = 1`, `mapFade = 0`, `questLogOpen = 0`) with no wasted top `"Dave's Map - "` title gap.
  - Above the map, clean **`WowStyle1DropdownTemplate`** dropdowns (`World/Continent`, `Zone`, and optional `Subzone/Floor`) match the rest of `Dave's Map` and `Dave's Sack`. Around the live map sits a delicate hairline **inner frame border** with subtle gold corner ticks; when the window is taller than the map's max height (`600px`), the map **centers vertically** over an unobtrusive, low-contrast, **subtly zone-themed backdrop** (`10` muted biome palettes matched to the active zone).
  - The slim `46px` skill-icon tab rail (`Base`, `Tracker & Log`, `Zone`, `Dungeon`) sits directly on the **left side of the text/content pane** (between the vertical map/panel divider and the searchable Quest Log / Zone / Dungeon / Quest Text area), with one shared top-right `X` close button.
  - Pressing `L` (`TOGGLEQUESTLOG`) or clicking the Quest Log micro-button opens the unified window right on the **`Tracker & Log`** tab.
  - Blizzard's map tracking & filter options (`Show Blizzard quest objectives`, `Show quest levels on map`, `Color quests by difficulty`, `Show Blizzard dungeon entrances`, `Track low-level quests`, `Show tracked content pins`) live cleanly under **Map Settings** (toolbar gear icon), the **`Base`** tab, and **`Esc > Options > AddOns > Dave's Map`**.
  - Out of the box, the World Map draws **only** the objective spawns (`...`) of quests you are actively tracking and the turn-in markers (`?`) of quests ready to turn in — keeping the map calm and uncluttered.
  - Optional non-quest POI categories (`Flight Master`, `Instance`, `Graveyard`, `Townsfolk & Services`, `Rare`, `Treasure & Lore`) default **on** and can be toggled all at once (or per group/category) via the right-aligned checkboxes at the top of **`Base`**, fading in and out smoothly with zoom distance so zoomed-out continent/world views never overload with icons. Hovering any **Rare** monster icon displays a rich **`Potential Known Drops:`** section with item icons, quality colours, slot/Lvl, and drop percentages.
  - A top-center **Zone & Coordinates Banner** shows the zone's difficulty-coloured level bracket and cursor/player coordinates.
- **Middle Tab Rail + Right Visual Content Pane (`Panel.lua` & `QuestDetails.lua`)**:
  - **2D Resizable OpenMap Viewport, Overflow Panel Growth & Unified Window Scale (Always Opens/Closes with the Map)**: Drag the bottom-right corner grip in 2D to resize both the live OpenMap viewport (within fixed `460..900 × 320..600` bounds, scaling smoothly to fit via OpenMap's built-in resize hooks) and the right content pane (`480px` minimum width, `516px` default); dragging past the map's max width or height keeps the map cleanly centered and scaled-to-fit over its subtle zone backdrop while growing the right content pane (`Base` / `Tracker & Log` / `Zone` / `Dungeon` / `Quest Text`) further. Drag the subtle vertical divider between the map and middle tab rail to trade width directly between them, or use **Window scale** (`50%`–`150%`) to scale the entire unified window together. Double-click the vertical divider or resize grip, click **`Reset Window Size`** inside **Map Settings**, or run `/dmap reset` (`/dmap dock`) to restore default sizes (`mapW`/`contentW`/`bodyH`).
  - **HUD Edit Mode Styling & Proper Right-Side Scrollbar (Same Look & Controls as `Dave's Sack`)**: Uses Blizzard's translucent dialog frame (`DialogBorderTranslucentTemplate`) across the unified window, tight left alignment on quest rows, Blizzard's native right-side **`MinimalScrollBar`** (wheel, arrows, draggable thumb, track click, auto-hiding when unscrollable), square beveled brass skill-icon tab buttons (`Media/slot.tga`, `tab_base.tga`, `tab_tracker.tga`, `tab_zone.tga`, `tab_dungeon.tga`, `back.tga`), 3-sliced beveled brass pill buttons (`Media/pill.tga`, `plus.tga`, `minus.tga`), and matching gold toolbar icon buttons (`findme.tga`, `waypoint.tga`, `gear.tga`) including two-column **Map Settings** (`/dmap style`) with draggable sliders (**Window scale** `50%`–`150%`, **Pin size** `50%`–`200%`, **Background** opacity `20%`–`100%`), Blizzard's **Background style** dropdown (`Dark`, `Light paper`, `Dark paper`, `Marble`, `Rock`), and **Map Tracking & Filters** checkboxes — all live-synced with **`Esc > Options > AddOns`**.
  - **`Base` Tab**: Toggles optional POI icon layers (`Locations & Travel`, `Townsfolk & Services`, `World & Rare Spawns` — all on by default, with right-side checkboxes on the top header and each group header to toggle all on/off) and embeds **Map Settings** and **Blizzard Map Tracking & Filters** directly in one place (`Alliance`/`Horde`/`Neutral` factions, `"Track only my zone's quests"`, `"Click a tracked quest to focus it"`, `"Show party quest numbers"`, `"Hide completed quests"`, `"Show the map layer"`, `"Show zone & coordinates banner"`, Blizzard tracking filters, and `Seamless & Dungeon Maps` options).
  - **`Tracker & Log` Tab (Merged Quest Log, Track Checkboxes & Drill-In Quest Text — Always Visible at Any Zoom)**:
    - Merges your complete Quest Log (with live `N / 25` fullness bar and clickable Objective Tracker **Track checkboxes** on every in-log quest row) into collapsible **`Focused`**, **`Ready to Turn In`**, **`Pinned`**, **`Tracking`**, and **`Quest Log`** sections — including sortable interactive rows for quests without QuestieDB pin data yet. Quests and chain steps you are currently **on** highlight in **gold**, the quest you **select** highlights in **green**, completed chain steps stay grey, and future unstarted chain steps display in clean neutral light text.
    - Clicking any in-log quest in **`Tracker & Log`** (or clicking a quest in Blizzard's **Objective Tracker** or **Ready to Turn In**, or right-clicking an in-log quest in **`Zone`** / **`Dungeon`**) drills straight into the built-in **Quest Text & Details** reader inside `Dave's Map` showing live objectives, completion text, clickable turn-in NPC link, full quest description, XP/coin/choice/fixed/currency/spell rewards with item tooltips, clickable chain steps, and **`< Back`**, **`Track`/`Untrack`**, **`Link`**, **`Share`**, and **`Abandon`** (with a taint-free HUD Edit Mode confirmation dialog).
    - Every tracked/pinned/ready/focused quest listed in **`Tracker & Log`** stays visible on the map at full opacity regardless of zoom level (all the way out to continent and world views), bypassing zoom-tier fades, viewport window culling, and faction checkboxes while optional Base POIs still fade cleanly with zoom.
    - While in a party or raid (when **`Show party quest numbers`** is enabled), each quest row displays a compact beveled brass number pill showing how many members of the group currently have that quest; hover the number pill to see a class-colored list of group members on that quest. Shift-click an in-log quest while chat is open to link it in chat (or Shift-click while chat is closed to drop a waypoint).
  - **`Zone` Tab (Zone Quests & Inline Quest Chains)**:
    - Always shows quests for the zone you're in or the zone you move the map to (switching back to **`Base`** returns the map to tracked objectives + checked optional POI layers).
    - **Sidebar Reference Precedence**: Clicking any quest, chain step, Starter link, or item reward inside **`Zone`** or **`Dungeon`** pins your current `Zone`/`Dungeon` list and markers (and suspends background host-map switching before the map moves) so flying to an out-of-zone pickup, objective, or turn-in never swaps what's viewable, flickers, or shifts rows. Navigating from Blizzard's real Quest Log / Objective Tracker, directly navigating on the World Map (clicking a zone/continent, right-clicking out to a parent map, dragging to another zone, or clicking **Find Me**), or clicking the active **`Zone`**/**`Dungeon`** tab again releases the lock and switches naturally.
    - Multi-step quest chains stay grouped **under the quest that started the chain**, rendered in a structured 4-column sortable grid (**`Quest` | `Starter` | `Lvl` | `XP`**) with clickable column headers to sort by **Level**, **XP**, **Starter**, or **Quest Name** (remembered across reloads). Each chained quest has a dedicated **`+`/`-` pill button** right beside the quest icon to easily expand or collapse its indented numbered bullet steps (`1.`, `2.`, `3.`, ...) right underneath, and each quest/step displays its own clickable **Starter** link column to fly straight to the pickup NPC/location.
    - Clicking a chained quest (or its `+`/`-` pill button) in `Dave's Map` expands or collapses its numbered bullet steps right underneath the starter quest (and chains you are currently on expand automatically): **completed steps are greyed out**, **uncompleted steps are green**, and **the focused step is highlighted**.
    - Clicking any quest mark on the World Map (available `!`, repeatable `!`, turn-in `?`, objective, completed checkmark, or Blizzard map quest pin) automatically opens the matching **`Zone`** (or **`Dungeon`**) tab, unfolds its section & chain if needed, highlights it, and scrolls straight to that quest.
    - **All item rewards across a chain** display right at the bottom of the chain when collapsed (tagged with step number), and move directly under the specific quest step that grants them when expanded (hover for full Blizzard item stats & gear comparison tooltip, Shift-click to link in chat).
  - **`Dungeon` Tab (Zone Dungeons, Floor Maps & Quest Starters)**:
    - Always shows the dungeon(s)/raid(s) in your current zone or the zone you move the map to (with total remaining XP, a 1-click **`<Dungeon> Map`** multi-floor map & boss-pin opener, and dungeon quest starters on the map), keeping chains collapsed by default with rewards shown below each quest or chain.
    - Clicking any item inside **`Dungeon`** pins that dungeon zone in context via the same sidebar precedence rule so flying to an out-of-zone pickup NPC never swaps away what's viewable, and releases automatically on direct World Map or real Quest Log navigation.
  - **Built-In Quest Text Inside the Window (Click Sound Only)**: Clicking any in-log quest in **`Tracker & Log`** (or right-clicking an in-log quest in **`Zone`** / **`Dungeon`**) plays only WoW's crisp UI click sound (`IG_MAINMENU_OPTION_CHECKBOX_ON`, never the paper page-flip sound) and opens our built-in **Quest Text & Details** reader directly inside `Dave's Map`'s right content pane.
  - **`< Back` Arrow & Search**: Toolbar **`< Back`** arrow button on the left beside the Search box (matching the height of the search bar & toolbar icons, plus **Mouse Button 4** anywhere on `Dave's Map`) restores your previous tab, Quest Text drill-in, reference layer, dungeon floor map, camera view, and scroll position; the search box searches titles, descriptions, zones, and reward item names.
- **Objective Tracker & Ready to Turn In (`Tracker.lua`)**:
  - Quests whose objectives are done move into a collapsible **Ready to Turn In** section below Blizzard's tracker. With **"Track only my zone's quests"** on, Blizzard's tracker follows quests for your current zone while a clickable **Pin** beside each tracked quest keeps pinned quests tracked across zones.
  - While in a party or raid (when **`Show party quest numbers`** is enabled), a matching taint-free brass number pill appears beside each tracked quest in Blizzard's Objective Tracker and in **Ready to Turn In** showing how many users in the group have the quest (updating live on `GROUP_ROSTER_UPDATE` and `UNIT_QUEST_LOG_CHANGED`); hover the number pill to see the class-colored names of group members on that quest.
  - Clicking a quest in the Objective Tracker or **Ready to Turn In** focuses its objectives/turn-in on the **Base** map layer, flies straight to its pin, and drills straight into its **Quest Text & Details** inside `Dave's Map`'s **`Tracker & Log`** tab (with `< Back` returning cleanly to the `Tracker & Log` list and **`Show All`** unfocusing back to all tracked quests).
- **The seamless map & dungeon maps** (`Stitch\` + `daves_map_textures`): every zone stitched into one continuous map with smooth cursor wheel zoom, progressive left-click zoom-in on empty map space all the way down to street level (and matching step-by-step right-click zoom-out), drag panning, middle-click / **Find Me** glide, Ctrl-click / **Drop Waypoint** (left-click or right-click a placed map pin to remove it without zooming out), and multi-floor dungeon & raid maps with boss pins.
- `/dmap` (or `/davesmap`) toggles the layer. Also `/dmap reset` (`/dmap dock`), `/dmap style` (`/dmap look`), `/dmap options`, `/dmap check [questID]`, `/dmap calib`, `/dmap zone`, `/dmap seamless`, and `/dmap version`. (`Dave's Map` opens and closes automatically with the World Map.)

## Companion Addons

### Quest & POI data: `daves_map_data` (QuestieDB Forever)

`wowaddons\daves_map_data\` (`Data.lua` + `NOTICE-QuestieDB.md`) is compiled by `tools\Update-MapData.py` from [Questie/QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB)'s WoW: Forever dataset (`data/Forever/`, `src/corrections/Forever/`, and `support/Forever/`). Everything fetched is parsed strictly as data in Python — nothing downloaded is ever executed.

```powershell
py -3 wowaddons\daves_map\tools\Update-MapData.py
```

### Seamless world & dungeon art: `daves_map_textures` (OpenMap)

`wowaddons\daves_map_textures\` (`Data\*.lua` + `Media\Textures\` + `LICENSE-OpenMap.txt` + `NOTICE-OpenMap.md`) is built by `tools\Update-MapTextures.py` from a local [OpenMap](https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap) WoW: Forever release (`v0.8.10`).

```powershell
py -3 wowaddons\daves_map\tools\Update-MapTextures.py [-Source <OpenMap folder or zip>]
```
